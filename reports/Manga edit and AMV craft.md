# Lock every hit to the beat grid

Manga edits, AMVs and short-form motion design are built the same way. The editor converts the song into a frame grid (**frames per beat = fps × 60 / BPM**, so 12 frames at 24 fps and 120 BPM), puts markers on it, and lands every hit (cut, flash, shake, zoom punch, velocity peak) on a marker frame. Everything between hits is shaped with easing so the motion snaps in and settles slowly. The best-documented recipes are concrete. A velocity ramp runs **200% speed at the beat, 20% between beats** while scale goes **100 → 130 → 100** ([AEJuice](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-do-velocity-edits-after-effects)). A flash is a **white solid 4-5 frames long that fades out** ([DVinfo](https://www.dvinfo.net/forum/archive/index.php/t-125973.html)). A shake is noise plus a periodic wave plus motion blur with mirrored edges ([Boris FX S_Shake](https://borisfx.com/documentation/sapphire/ae/shake/)). Still manga panels come alive when they are cut into depth planes that move at different speeds ([AEJuice parallax](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-create-parallax-effect-in-after-effects)). One gap needs stating up front: **no indexable source describes the specific styles of Haiko, Gokuponch, Bri or Mutioke**. Searches turned up only unrelated manga titles and CapCut templates, so creator-specific signatures still have to be logged by stepping through their videos frame by frame. The recipes below are the genre's shared grammar. Values marked *starting point* are practitioner conventions that no fetched source confirmed. Tune them by eye.

## The beat grid comes first: markers, 4-beat phrases, and a 2-3 frame correction

Every pro workflow starts by expanding the audio waveform and placing markers on the beat peaks. Speed and scale changes are then keyed between those markers ([AEJuice](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-do-velocity-edits-after-effects)). Markers tapped live during playback **land 2-3 frames late**, so editors nudge them afterwards ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/key-frames-on-audio-beats/m-p/12538705/highlight/true)). In AWIeffect the cleanest fix is to compute the grid from BPM and snap markers to it. At 30 fps one beat is 1800/BPM frames, so 128 BPM gives about 14 frames ([Morphic](https://morphic.com/resources/videos/lyric-videos)). The r/amv "scene shifting" guidance adds two structural rules. **Alternate long shots with very short ones**, and remember that beats fall in **groups of 4**, which sets the phrase lengths for cuts. A quick white flash hides a cut to a still frame ([r/amv mirror](https://reddit.rtrace.io/r/amv/comments/xjbhtg/scene_shifting_the_easiest_mistake_all_video)).

| Section | Shot rhythm | FX density (*starting point*) |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | Long holds, slow push-ins | Minimal; grade and grain only |
| Build | One cut per beat, ramps accelerating | Small shakes, light zoom punches |
| Drop | A hit on every beat, strobe on downbeats | Flashes, impact frames, big shakes, velocity |
| Outro | Return to long holds | Fade or freeze |

Hits go on the marker frame, or 0-2 frames before the transient so they *read* as on beat (*starting point*). The section map above is genre convention. No fetched source published bar counts from named editors.

## Five hit recipes carry most edits: velocity, shake, flash, zoom punch, glitch

**Velocity.** Keyframe speed at 200% on marker A, 20% between markers and 200% on marker B. Key scale 100 → 130 → 100 on the same frames. Apply easing, refine in the graph editor and turn on motion blur ([AEJuice](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-do-velocity-edits-after-effects)). The classic bug is the stall. Easy Ease on time remap drops speed to 0 and then jumps to 1 sec/sec. The fix is to set the outgoing velocity of the first key and the incoming velocity of the second key to **1 sec/sec at 33.33% influence** ([Creative Cow](https://creativecow.net/?p=7479)). CapCut exposes the same idea as speed-curve presets (Montage, Hero, Bullet, Flash In/Out) with a custom curve of 5 beat points ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/how-to-do-velocity-on-capcut)). The "velocity x flash x shake x reverse" combination is a standing template trend ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/explore/velocity-x-flash-x-shake-x-reverse)).

**Shake.** Sapphire S_Shake is the clearest reference to rebuild. Its defaults are **Amplitude 1, Frequency 8**, Rand amp X 0.2 / Y 0.1, a periodic Wave component (Wave Freq 0.5 cycles/s), motion blur length 1 and **Reflect** edge wrap. Its styles are Normal, Twitchy (Stillness 0.7, so the shake fires only part of the time) and Jumpy (Drift 0.3). Per-channel R/G/B amplitude and phase give a chromatic shake ([Boris FX](https://borisfx.com/documentation/sapphire/ae/shake/)). The wiggle shorthand gives the feel. **wiggle(10,3)** is a fast, tight jitter and **wiggle(2,40)** is a slow, dramatic sway ([CG Director](https://www.cgdirector.com/after-effects-wiggle-expression)). Alight Motion's Oscillate builds a shake transition by keying Magnitude to or from 0, with Angle 0° for horizontal and 90° for vertical ([Alight Motion guide](https://guide.alightmotion.com/effects/oscillate)). *Starting point* for an impact shake at 1080p: 4-8 frames that start at peak (20-60 px, 1-4° rotation) and decay to 0 with an ease-out, at about 12-24 Hz. For handheld drift, use 1-3 Hz at 5-15 px. Always overscale or mirror the edges.

**Flash.** A white solid starts on the marker and lasts 4-5 frames with a fade, and blend modes are worth trying ([DVinfo](https://www.dvinfo.net/forum/archive/index.php/t-125973.html)). Impact-frame packs pair **"Darken Background" with "Flash Frame"** ([Panther Dynamics](https://pantherdynamics.yolasite.com/panther-dynamics-blog/page/2)). Impact frames are defined by appearing "for a split second" with sharp contrast and exaggerated poses ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/cultures/impact-frames)). *Starting points*: 1-2 frame hard flashes for strobes, 4-8 frame Add/Screen flashes that decay, 1-2 frame black dips for heavy hits, and 2-4 frames of inverted or red/black silhouette for impact frames.

**Zoom punch and zoom transition.** Scaling exposes black edges. Motion Tile with a larger output (150-300%) and **Mirror Edges**, plus motion blur, hides them ([AEJuice transitions](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-design-dynamic-transitions-between-clips-in-adobe-after-effects); [Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/mirror-edges-not-working/m-p/11946662)). *Starting points*: for a punch, scale 100 → 110-130% over 2-4 frames and ease back over 6-12. For a transition, clip A goes to 200-400% over 4-8 frames ending on the cut and clip B comes in from 30-50% (or 200%) to 100, with a hard ease into the cut.

**Glitch.** Video Copilot Twitch fires random operators (Blur, Color, Light, Scale, Slide, Time) at a "twitch interval", each with Speed, Amount and Seed. Slide includes an RGB split ([Video Copilot](https://www.videocopilot.net/products/twitch/features)). Universe Glitch Transition keys **Mix** for duration and adds RGB Split Max, Size and Displacement ([Maxon](https://help.maxon.net/rg/en-us/Content/html/glitch-transition.html)). *Starting point* for a rebuild: R offset +X and B offset −X (5-30 px) for 2-6 frames on the hit, plus random block-slice displacement re-seeded every 1-2 frames.

## Easing decides whether motion feels cheap or After Effects grade

Use the speed graph to shape acceleration (best for position) and the value graph to judge values (easier for scale) ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/after-effects-graph-editor-problem/m-p/10552536/highlight/true)). Easy Ease is **speed 0 at 33.33% influence**. That is a neutral default, and some editors switch to **60%** for a snappier move ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/changing-the-ease-default-values/m-p/10906650)). Overshoot uses the inertial bounce expression. Animoplex defaults are **amp 5, freq 2, decay 4**, and amp 0.05 / freq 4 / decay 8 gives a fast bounce. Raise decay to 5-6 if the bounce lingers ([Animoplex gist](https://gist.github.com/jexchan/bbff1a3c9701ce5fd4db6f07b2f78d6d); [Tuts+](https://photography.tutsplus.com/tutorials/how-to-animate-inertial-bounce-expressions-in-after-effects--cms-41469)). Contact keys on a bounce get **0% influence**, which gives a hard contact ([Adobe community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/help-with-speed-animation-in-graph-editor/m-p/11757827)). Motion-design timing guides run exits at about **75% of the entrance duration** and note that ease-out feels faster than ease-in at the same length ([impeccable reference](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@nqlib/nqui@0.6.3/docs/nqui-skills/impeccable/reference/motion-design.md)). At 30 fps, that puts title entrances at roughly 8-15 frames and exits at 6-10.

| Feel | Curve (*starting point*, except where cited) |
|---|---|
| Neutral | 33% / 33% (AE default, cited) |
| Punch-in / impact | Launch 0-30%, landing 70-90% (fast start, long settle) |
| Zoom into cut | Ease-in heavy: slow start, fastest on the cut frame |
| Velocity whip | Narrow speed spike, both handles 80-100% |
| Pop / logo | Overshoot 5-15% past target (0 → 110 → 97 → 100), 4-6 f per segment |
| Anticipation | 2-4 f pull-back of 3-10% before the main move |

## Still panels animate through depth planes, paper texture and per-beat life

The documented core is to split the panel into planes and move the background slower than the foreground. Studios call this "the multiple planes technique" ([AEJuice](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-create-a-parallax-effect-in-after-effects); [ArtStation](https://gustozoh.artstation.com/projects/n00DzE)). In a 3D camera setup, set the layers at different Z values, use a 50 mm camera with Depth of Field and link focus to the subject layer. Aperture sets blur strength ([AEJuice DOF](https://aejuice.com/blog/how-to-create-depth-of-field-in-after-effects)). Mobile editors cut the layers in Ibis Paint X and animate them in Alight Motion. Desktop editors use Photoshop with After Effects ([fbpe tutorial list](https://old.fbpe.org/manga-animation-tutorial-alight-motion.html)). The full workflow below is genre convention that no transcript confirmed. Clean the scan and remove bubbles. Cut the character, the FX (debris, speed lines) and the background into layers, and fill the hole behind the character with cloned screentone. Push in across the shot (100 → 110%) with the foreground moving faster. Add mesh warp sway on hair and cloth, eye glows and dust. Overlay scrolling speed lines plus paper grain or halftone so it still reads as print. Then hit the beats with the recipes above. *Starting points* for 3D depth: background at Z +1000 to 3000, midground at 0, foreground at −300 to −600. Compensate scale as **100 × distance / zoom** so each layer keeps its framing. Drift pushes travel 200-600 px over 30-90 frames, impact pushes travel 100-300 px in 4-8 frames with shake, and orbits rotate a null 10-30° on Y over 1-2 s. For a dolly zoom, key camera Z and zoom in opposite directions so the subject's size stays constant. Faked in 2D, it needs real camera motion to read ([Lester Banks](https://lesterbanks.com/2012/07/creating-a-dolly-zoom-vertigo-effect-in-a-timelapse-shot-using-after-effects/)).

## Type, grade and layout follow a "one loud thing per frame" rule

Text reveals are one animator (Opacity, Position, Scale or Character Offset) driven by a keyed Range Selector. Set **Smoothness 0** for a crisp typewriter ([Pixel Bakery](https://pixelbakery.com/education/tutorials/how-to-add-typewriter-effect)). Ease High/Low shape each character's transition separately from how fast the selector sweeps ([ProVideo Coalition](https://www.provideocoalition.com/cmg_hidden_gems_chapter_21_textacy/)). Per-character 3D plus Rotation animators give letter flips ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/using/examples-resources-text-animation.html)). Lyric timing counts hold time from the moment a word stops moving. A rule of thumb is about 1 s for a short word and 2-3 s for a 4-8 word phrase, with one word per beat or a scale-up on the chorus ([Morphic](https://morphic.com/resources/videos/lyric-videos)). *Starting points*: stagger 1-2 frames per character or 2-4 per word, use blur-in (Blur 20-40), masked slide-ups and scramble reveals, and keep the original Japanese SFX animated with a scale pop and shake.

For 9:16 at 1080×1920, keep faces and titles inside about **870×1310**. That means clearing about 130 px at the top, 480 px at the bottom and 150 px on the right for TikTok, and about 180 px on the right for Reels ([Moda](https://moda.app/tools/story-safe-zone-checker)). Pair one loud display face with one quiet face and use 1-2 accent colors. Bold weights survive fast scaling, and headlines should be at least 36 px ([Oakgen](https://oakgen.ai/blog/kinetic-typography-without-after-effects)). For grading, set **Levels first** (black, mid and white points) rather than crude curves or exposure, because rough corrections make edits look "weird" ([AMV tutorial summary](https://youtubesummary.com/summary/DY14ugv1wd8)). Animate Levels to match brightness between shots and Curves to match color ([Michael DeVowe](https://vimeo.com/14396098)). Glow stacks in two passes, one tight (threshold around 30, radius around 5) and one wide ([Tuts+](https://photography.tutsplus.com/courses/create-a-stranger-things-inspired-text-animation-in-adobe-after-effects/lessons/how-to-add-color-and-glow-effects)). Deep Glow uses a 100-300 px radius with exposure kept below clipping, and needs Grow Bounds at about 2× the glow extent ([Toolfarm](https://www.toolfarm.com/?p=22323)). On anime, correct casts with luminance and not hue-only tools ([Doom9](https://forum.doom9.org/archive/index.php/t-167027.html)). *Starting point* for the stack: Levels → gentle S curve → vibrance +10-30 → LUT at 30-70% → two glows → grain and vignette, with glow or exposure spiking on hits.

## Conclusion

The craft comes down to three moves. Quantize to the beat grid, give every hit an asymmetric ease (fast attack, long decay) plus motion blur and mirrored edges, and gate the intensity by song section so the drop has somewhere to go. For AWIeffect, every key effect is buildable from the documented recipes. S_Shake maps to per-axis noise, a sine wave, a stillness gate and edge reflect. Twitch maps to random operators fired at an interval with a seed. Velocity maps to a speed curve that hands off at 1× speed so it never stalls. Those features matter more than any one creator's preset.

The biggest unknown is the named creators themselves. Haiko, Gokuponch, Bri and Mutioke left no text trail, and the scene's numbers live inside videos and shared Alight Motion links. One source warns that those "copy the link" preset schemes are a common malware vector ([search result](https://postecards.poste.it/bold-stats/alight-motion-shakes-get-the-ialight-link-1767648748)). The next research step is primary: step through 3-5 edits per creator frame by frame, then log hit lengths, flash types and shake decay against the beat grid to turn the *starting point* values here into measured presets.
